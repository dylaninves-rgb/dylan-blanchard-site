How it started

“I found myself needing to take on some creative passion or pursuit, and I was given the opportunity as well as an experience I couldn’t exactly translate myself. And so I translated it through art.”

The work

My work is about the different ways I felt disconnected or separate from those experiences that are right nearby.

“The feelings that are in the back door, the things that just get unsaid, the faces that get looked at but not seen. So it’s about expressing and finding the deep parts of myself and others while also trying to find my way in the world.”

Influences

“I can’t think of many influences by name, but I would be able to recognise some of the work if I saw it. My work tends to be so raw, abstract and intuitive that it’s hard for me to find someone that I can truly connect with artistically.”
