About

Bylan Blanchard is a painter working primarily in oil and mixed media, in bodies of work rather than single images. The paintings return to a small set of questions — how identity holds together under observation, what desire and insecurity do to perception, and how memory rewrites itself in the retelling — without much interest in answering them cleanly.

The work draws on classical painting and architecture, anime, film, and abstraction in roughly equal measure, more for their handling of figure and structure than for any single visual style. Each series is its own extended argument; individual paintings are read as chapters, not standalone statements.
