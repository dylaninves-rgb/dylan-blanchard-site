About

An experience I couldn’t translate

“I found myself needing to take on some creative passion or pursuit, and I was given the opportunity as well as an experience I couldn’t exactly translate myself. And so I translated it through art.”

The work is raw, abstract and intuitive — made by feel rather than from reference. Figures and faces arrive out of the paint and go back into it.
